## 8. Questions for WP0.3 (JSON Schemas)

1. How does a probe-result evidence record point to its probe? A `probe` field on evidence, or only through the `probe-result` file? (The plan example used `source: probe`, which I removed.)
2. `representative`: should the schema take YAML booleans `true | false` plus the string `unknown`, or three strings? The vocabulary block holds strings because unquoted `true` parses as a boolean.
3. Layer status: plan section 5 says `draft | reviewed | deprecated` for entities and layers, but WP3.1 says layers have `draft | reviewed`. Which one?
4. Is `subject_scope` on an entity authored (as in the plan's `orders.yaml` example) or derived (spec principle 2: "scopes ... are computed")? I wrote it as an authored claim checked against the computed closure.
5. Are evidence records inline in entity files (plan example), standalone in `evidence/`, or both? The `evidence` file kind suggests both.
6. Do the proposed register statuses and default ranks (section 6) hold?
7. Should probes after M1 (current-row ties, value set) be added to `probe_kind` now, so the schema need not change later?
8. Should `environment` and `tenant` have their own blocks of reserved values, or stay free slugs?
