## 5. Confirmed / inferred / unknown

**Confirmed**

- On the same synthetic rows, SQLite and PostgreSQL agree on every neutral snapshot part (names, nullability, type classes, constraints) and on all 45 probe counts; PostgreSQL enforces its constraints and SQLite does not, as the vocabulary says.
- The DDL snapshot (git adapter) agrees with the SQLite catalog on every neutral part.

**Inferred**

- The retail profile needed a rule for reference-to-reference links: Chinook has eight of them, and without the rule they were residue. Labelled `reference_data` ("about no customer").
- Entities stay `status: draft`: the owner has not reviewed them in a report yet; `reviewed` is the owner's call.
- The `chinook-ddl` source reads the working tree (`revision: worktree`), because the DDL lands in the same commit; its pins are blob hashes, valid in this commit and later.
- Probe evidence is `data_quality: synthetic`, environment `local`. Zero-violation results carry over to production (spec), so they confirm the facts.

**Unknown**

- Whether the hand-written DDL differs from the upstream Chinook files in details (column lengths, constraint names). It follows the public description; nothing was compared against the upstream files.
