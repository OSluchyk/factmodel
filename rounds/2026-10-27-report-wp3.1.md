## 5. Confirmed / inferred / unknown

**Confirmed**

- No model in the repository had a layer, so the 2.0.0 change migrates nothing. Both examples validate, and `fact migrate` reports them at 2.0.0.
- Each `layers` rule fails on its own (unit tests); the gate fixture fails only `layers`.

**Inferred**

- **Distinct rule.** The concept form `distinct` is written into D-019, beyond the brief's "value vocabulary or predicate". F-0001 (repeated area codes) needs a rule no predicate can express, since the duplicate rows are identical. The brief's section 4 asks for exactly that as a `default_filters` entry.
- **Brief keys, written early.** The measure keys `suppressed` and `companion` and the vocabulary `suppressed_handling` come from the brief's round-22 text. They are in the v1 shape now, so round 22 needs no further contract change.
- **Hazard inheritance.** A hazard is inherited when it applies to an entity or relationship the layer reads, or is listed on the entity, on a field the layer uses, or on a joined relationship.
- **Gate placement.** ID resolution stays in the loader (`references`, blocking); the semantic rules are the non-blocking `layers` stage.
- **Corpus location.** `corpus` keeps its object form (`location`, `pin`, `synthetic`); `location` is relative to the layer folder.

**Unknown**

- None for this round.
