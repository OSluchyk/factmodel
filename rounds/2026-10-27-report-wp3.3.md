## 5. Confirmed / inferred / unknown

**Confirmed**

- Both layers are proven on SQLite and DuckDB with the same outcome per item; the committed reports equal a fresh run.
- Each of the six naive queries gives a different answer from its reference on the seed (tested).

**Inferred**

- Only `same_rows` and `same_predicate` count towards the share, over items without `known_gap` (D-021 as pre-approved).
- `same_predicate` references are the consumer's own form, with conjuncts reordered and identifiers quoted: they test that the filter is the one the layer demands, not that SQL text matches.
- **Simulated owner act.** `fact equivalence accept` was run by the agent for both layers because the brief says the owner's act is simulated once here. Future accepts are the owner's.
- **Where the consumers needed more than the layer.** The QCEW consumer derives `agglvl_code` from the A4 matrix, and both consumers hard-code the join conditions behind relationship keys. These are the gaps the dogfood note lists.

**Unknown**

- How a non-reference consumer (an LLM agent, for example) would score on these corpora; M4's pilot can tell.

## 6. Divergences
