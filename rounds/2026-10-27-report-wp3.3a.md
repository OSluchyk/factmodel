## 5. Confirmed / inferred / unknown

**Confirmed**

- Rollup counts on SQLite, PostgreSQL and DuckDB:
  - county to state, third-month employment: 296 of 296 clean parents exact, 24 parents with a suppressed child;
  - NAICS parent: 216 of 216 exact, 8 suppressed parents with disclosed children;
  - annual average employment, county to state: 47 exact and 27 within rounding of 74, none beyond.
- Every earlier QCEW probe result is unchanged by the new seed. Those probes count rows, keys and domains, not measure values.

**Inferred**

- Rollups are declared on layer measures (`rollups`); they need a measure, which only a layer has. The key is in D-020 and is additive (2.1.0).
- `required` concepts are applied to a measure (in a filter or a conditional aggregate). The companion `suppressed_cells` counts the rows the `disclosed` concept leaves out of the same selection.
- The brief's `area_kind` concept became three predicate concepts (county, state, MSA): a concept has one predicate. Likewise `period` became the quarter field; the year is part of the grain and of every request.
- `distinct_areas` is a required concept on `area_titles`; its `distinct` form is the one D-019 added for this case.

**Unknown**

- None for this round.
